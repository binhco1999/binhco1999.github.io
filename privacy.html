Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how Auto Video Maker (“the app”), a personal tool operated by the owner of the EngToEnd channels (home page, YouTube @engtoend), handles information. The app is used only by its owner to create short videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube, Facebook and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: the app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope, which lets it upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, store or modify any other YouTube or Google account data, and it does not access viewers' data.
  • Other platforms: when the owner connects Facebook or TikTok, the app uses the permissions needed to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own Page or account, and nothing else.
  • Content: the text and images the owner sends to the app are used only to create the owner's videos.

2. How information is used

Access tokens are used solely to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with third parties.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads go through the YouTube API Services and are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Storage and security

Credentials (OAuth refresh tokens) are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private infrastructure (GitHub Actions secrets and a private database) and are never published. Generated videos are kept in the owner's private storage only as long as needed to publish them.

4. Viewers

The app does not collect, store or process any personal information about people who watch the published videos. Interactions with the videos (views, likes, comments) are handled by each platform under its own privacy policy.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in the security settings of each platform. After revocation the app can no longer upload videos.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Questions can be sent to tranbinhco.work@gmail.com.
